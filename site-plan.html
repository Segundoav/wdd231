Individual Project Site Plan

Prepared by: Segundo Demetrio Alvarez Vilca

1. Site Name

Name Selection: Seguros Activos

Explanation: I selected "Seguros Activos" (Active Insurance) because it portrays a protective, modern, and proactive approach to insurance brokering. It emphasizes that safety solutions are continuously running to protect clients, vehicles, and families in Lima, Peru.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of "Seguros Activos" is to deliver an accessible digital gateway for independent insurance brokerage. It serves as a central hub where local clients can easily review, contrast, and request personalized coverage plans across four critical pillars: Automobile, Health, Home, and Life insurance, backed by dynamic JSON data structures.

3. Scenarios (Target Audience Persona Questions)

  • Scenario 1: "I recently purchased a new vehicle in Lima. What is the most effective auto insurance policy available within my price range, and what specific incidents does it protect against?"
  • Scenario 2: "How can I contact an independent broker directly to obtain a customized, combined medical health insurance plan for my family members?"

4. Color Palette

The selected palette ensures high visual contrast and matches accessibility requirements (WebAIM compliant):

Primary Color: Deep Blue (#0B2545)
Applied to: Document headers, primary section headings, navigation blocks, and structural branding elements.
Secondary Color: Golden Yellow (#EEB902)
Applied to: Buttons, links, dynamic accents, and interactive Call-To-Action (CTA) interfaces.

5. Typography

Headings (H1, H2, H3)

Font Family: Montserrat, Helvetica, sans-serif.

Application: Used exclusively for titles to command authority, clarity, and clean organizational structures.

Body Content

Font Family: Open Sans, Arial, sans-serif.

Application: Used for all narrative paragraphs, listed items, and form inputs to ensure top-tier legibility on high-density screen displays.
